The Real Cost of “Good Enough” Fasteners

Many contractors and buyers default to traditional mechanical anchors—wedge bolts, sleeve anchors, or drop-in anchors—assuming they offer the best strength. But these solutions come with hidden costs:

  • Time-consuming installation: Multiple steps (drilling, cleaning, inserting, setting) slow down crews.
  • Installation sensitivity: Poor hole cleaning or incorrect torque can reduce load capacity by up to 40% .
  • Limited design flexibility: Large edge distances and spacing requirements restrict layout options.
  • Corrosion vulnerability: Uncoated carbon steel fails prematurely in humid or coastal environments.

These issues lead to delays, rework, safety incidents, and warranty claims—costs that far exceed any initial savings on fastener price.

FONGPREAN’s Answer: Patented Design, Proven Performance

Our concrete screws are built on decades of fastener expertise and real-world feedback from contractors and engineers. Key innovations include:

  • Advanced Thread Geometry: Inspired by undercut and dual-lead threadforms, our screws cut precise mating threads directly into concrete during installation. This creates a mechanical interlock that delivers exceptional pull-out and shear resistance—even in cracked concrete.
  • One-Step Installation: No anchors, no adhesives, no setting tools. Drill, clear dust, and drive. Installation is up to 60% faster than traditional methods .
  • Corrosion Protection: Choose from:
    • Carbon steel with high-performance anti-corrosion coating (ideal for indoor use)
    • A2 (304) or A4 (316) stainless steel for harsh or marine environments
  • Global Compliance: Products meet ISO 898, EN 14592, and can be certified for seismic zones per local building codes.

Real-World Result: In a recent industrial retrofit in Southern Taiwan, a client replaced wedge anchors with FONGPREAN M10×80mm concrete screws to mount heavy-duty equipment brackets. The team installed 1,200 fixings in two days—a task projected to take four days using conventional anchors. Load testing confirmed 100% compliance, with zero failures.
