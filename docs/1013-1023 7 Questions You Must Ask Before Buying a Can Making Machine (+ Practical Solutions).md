## **The 7 Key Procurement Questions and Solutions**

### **1. What Production Capacity Do I Really Need?**

**Buyer Concern:**  
“We want to expand capacity, but we’re unsure what output range our line should target.”

**Expert Explanation:**  
Production capacity depends on several factors: can size, material thickness, production shifts, and market demand. Overspecifying capacity increases investment costs; underspecifying it leads to bottlenecks.

**Practical Solution:**  
Start by calculating your daily and annual can output targets. For example, if your plant operates two 8-hour shifts at 85% efficiency, multiply that by the machine’s stroke rate to estimate needed throughput. Discuss this data with your **can making machine manufacturer** to match the right model — for instance, a **high-speed necking machine** or a **multi-station flanger** designed for your volume and can diameter range.

### **2. How Do I Ensure Equipment Compatibility with My Existing Line?**

**Buyer Concern:**  
“Our current line includes older machines — will new ones integrate smoothly?”

**Expert Explanation:**  
In complex **can making equipment** setups, incompatibility between new and existing systems can cause misalignment, uneven flow, or control errors.

**Practical Solution:**  
Request **technical layout drawings** and, ideally, a 3D line simulation before purchase. Ensure the new machine’s PLC, electrical voltage, and control interfaces are compatible with your current automation platform.
